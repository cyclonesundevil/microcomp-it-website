Physics / Model

Inspiral: quasi-circular quadrupole energy loss with 1PN and 1.5PN flux corrections; fGW = 2forbit.

Late inspiral / merger: smooth EOB-inspired plunge interpolation beginning at 6 GM/c². It is not numerical relativity.

Remnant: compact nonspinning numerical-relativity-inspired mass and spin fits.

Ringdown: fitted Kerr l=m=2 fundamental quasi-normal mode, h ∝ exp(−t/τ) cos(2πft+φ).

Strain assumes a reference distance of 400 Mpc. Object sizes, spatial distance, time playback, and strain display use separate visual transforms.

Wave propagation: fronts are emitted at fixed GW-phase intervals and advance by c(t−temit). Their scene-space radius is compressed, but emission timing and physical propagation are not.

Visual layer: the grid is a two-dimensional reference lattice for in-plane tidal stretch/compression—not a sagging membrane or literal extra dimension. Its shader deformation, quadrupolar lobes, common-horizon morph, power-driven flash, and shape oscillation are amplified teaching graphics driven by the physical state—not additional field-equation solutions.

Inspiral

The black holes lose orbital energy and angular momentum through gravitational radiation. Separation falls while orbital frequency rises.

Chirp

Gravitational-wave frequency and amplitude rise rapidly as the horizons approach.

Merger

The horizons combine and the system emits its strongest gravitational radiation.

Ringdown

The distorted remnant settles toward a Kerr black hole through damped quasi-normal-mode radiation.

Mass sets physical scale

One gravitational radius is GM/c². Increasing mass enlarges the horizon and ISCO in kilometers, but does not change their dimensionless radii.

Spin moves the ISCO

The prograde Kerr ISCO contracts from 6 r_g at zero spin toward the horizon as spin approaches its theoretical limit.

Readouts share one reference

Clock ratio and gravitational redshift are evaluated at the prograde ISCO. They are model quantities relative to a distant observer, not measurements of a particular real disk.

Angle changes appearance

The brightness ratio uses a simplified special-relativistic Doppler estimate. Exact images require ray tracing, radiative transfer, and a physical accretion-flow model.

Model boundary

Assumptions: an uncharged Kerr black hole, dimensionless spin 0 ≤ a* < 1, and a prograde equatorial test particle at the ISCO. Calculations use G = c = M = 1 before converting lengths with r_g = GM/c². The colored disk, photon ring, light paths, and spacetime well are qualitative.
